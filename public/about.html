About ShadowTrace

Built to close the speed gap

Criminal groups move fast because they're built for advantage. Institutions move carefully because they're built for process. That gap is where evidence disappears and cases go cold. We exist to close it.

Our Mission

Crypto-enabled crime is growing at over 300% year-on-year. Criminals exploit pseudonymous addresses, cross-chain bridges, and privacy protocols to move stolen funds faster than institutions can respond. By the time evidence packages are assembled, the money has often disappeared.

We believe investigators shouldn't need to be blockchain experts to follow the money. ShadowTrace was built to give the people on the front line — detectives, compliance officers, regulators — the speed to act while evidence is still fresh and the rigour to make it stand up in court.

Our mission: close the gap between how fast crime moves and how fast institutions can respond. Every feature we build is measured against one question — does this help an investigator get to the truth faster?
