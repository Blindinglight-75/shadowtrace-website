01 // WHY SHADOWTRACE

Built for investigators. Not retrofitted for them.

Most blockchain analytics platforms were designed for compliance teams at cryptocurrency exchanges — built to satisfy regulators, not to build criminal cases. ShadowTrace takes a fundamentally different approach. Every feature, workflow, and output has been purpose-built for the demands of criminal investigation, from initial intelligence through to courtroom disclosure.

02 // THE PROBLEM

The problem with legacy platforms

Built for a different buyer

Legacy tools were designed for compliance teams at exchanges, not field investigators working criminal cases. The workflows, terminology, and outputs reflect that origin.

Black-box risk scores

Opaque scoring algorithms that can't explain why an address was flagged. Scores that don't hold up when a defence barrister asks "how did you reach that conclusion?"

Procurement, not deployment

Enterprise sales cycles that take months, licensing models that lock out smaller agencies, and onboarding timelines that miss the investigative window entirely.

03 // OUR APPROACH

The ShadowTrace approach

Case-First Design

Every feature starts with the investigative question. What address is this? Where did the funds go? Who controls this cluster? The interface follows the logic of a criminal investigation, not a compliance workflow.

Field-to-Courtroom

From the first address lookup to the final evidence package, ShadowTrace maintains a complete audit trail. Every step is timestamped, reproducible, and ready for disclosure.

Agentic AI

Intelligent automation that handles the tedious parts — tracing through peel chains, identifying mixer patterns, clustering related wallets — so investigators can focus on the decisions that matter. Every AI output includes a clear explanation of its reasoning.
